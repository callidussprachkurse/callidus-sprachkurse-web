The Gap

Training perfects the voice.
It does not teach the career.

How do you find representation? How do you negotiate? How do you win an audition? How do you truly inhabit a role, so that meaning, mood and intention ring true? That is the work of the month.

I

Career Craft

Finding representation, negotiating engagements, professional auditioning and self-presentation — the business of a singing life.

II

Interpretation

Role and repertoire preparation in the original language, so that mood, intonation and dramatic meaning are true — not merely performed.

III

Foundation

Music theory and history — the bedrock that turns a fine voice into an informed, complete artist.

IV

Masterclasses

Time with leading figures of the profession, drawn from our network of respected coaches and artists.

The Programme

Four weeks, planned to the hour.

Three weeks in the heart of Germany, one week in Italy’s Tuscany region. Individual lessons every morning, repertoire and industry in the afternoon, rehearsals and auditions towards the end — closing with a concert on a real stage.

    Who It’s For

    Master’s and post-graduate opera singers.

    Emerging artists at the threshold — completing or just beyond their formal training, ready to make the leap into the profession. A small, hand-picked cohort, welcomed from anywhere in the world; the stage is in Europe.

    Admission by selectionECP-Arts is deliberately exclusive. A maximum of ten places per edition, awarded after a personal review of recordings and résumé — and an interview. We would rather leave a place empty than fill it.

    Répétiteurs and emerging opera managers: a few places are open on separate terms — write to us.

    The Difference

    A name the industry will know.

    ECP-Arts does not represent artists and is not an agency. Instead, we become a trusted interface: those we train arrive prepared, ready and connected.

    Agencies and houses come to recognise what an ECP-Arts singer brings to the room — a reputation earned through results, not a certificate.

    The Fee

    One figure. No second bill.

    for the month · per participant

    One figure, paid once, covers the month itself — the teaching, a single bed, meals, the travel we organise, the stage. What it cannot cover is what happens before you arrive and after you leave.

    In the fee

    • iIndividual lessons, coaching and masterclasses
    • iiAccommodation for the full month, Germany and Italy
    • iiiMeals
    • ivAll travel within Europe during the month, Germany to Italy included — with checked baggage up to 23 kg (50 lb)
    • vRehearsals, the public concert and professional filming

    You arrange yourself

    • iYour journey to the programme and back home
    • iiVisa fees (Schengen, type C), paid at the German embassy in your country
    • iiiHealth insurance — compulsory; we can put you in touch with partners
    • ivScores and personal spending money
    • vOpera tickets — should you wish to attend a performance during the month, we are glad to arrange it, but the cost of the tickets is yours to bear

    Applying is free. The fee falls due only after a place has been offered and accepted; payment terms are set out in the participation contract. Répétiteurs and emerging opera managers are quoted separately.
