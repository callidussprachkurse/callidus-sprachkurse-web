Not a conservatoire. Not a degree.
ECPA begins where your training ended.

We do not award degrees, diplomas or certificates. ECPA is for artists who have already completed their training — and now need the one thing the academy never taught: the profession itself.

The Gap

Training perfects the craft. It does not teach the career.

How to find representation. How to negotiate. How to command an audition or an interview. How to truly inhabit the work — so meaning, mood and intention ring true. That is the work of ECPA.

01

Career

Finding representation, negotiating engagements, professional auditioning and self-marketing — the business of the profession.

02

Interpretation

Understanding the work in its original language, so that mood, intonation and dramatic meaning are true — not merely performed.

03

Foundation

Music theory and history — the bedrock that turns fine technique into an informed, complete artist.

04

Masterclasses

Time with leading figures of the industry, drawn from our network of the world’s most respected coaches.

★  The Showcase

Every trimester closes on a real stage.

A public performance at a partner venue across Europe — your work seen by an audience, by agencies and houses, and captured on film. Not an exam. A debut.

The Difference

A name the industry knows.

ECPA does not represent artists and is not an agency. Instead, we become a trusted interface: those we train arrive prepared, ready and connected.

Agencies and houses come to recognise what an ECPA artist brings to the room — a reputation earned through results, not a certificate.

  • Prepared, audition-ready professionals
  • Direct exposure to agencies and houses at every showcase
  • A network that opens doors, in Europe and beyond

“It is one thing to perform the notes. It is another to know the story — and make an audience believe it.”

The interpretive depth that separates a trained talent from a working artist sits at the heart of everything we teach.

A three-month European residency.

Preparation in Germany. A furnished home in a vibrant district. A final performance on a real European stage — celebrated, filmed, and seen by the people who matter.

The Residency

Train in Europe. Perform on its stages.

Each cohort is small and selective. You live and work alongside fellow professionals from around the world, coached intensively, and finish on stage — where careers are noticed.

  • One trimester · three immersive months
  • Housing included — live in the heart of the city
  • Culminating performance at a partner venue
